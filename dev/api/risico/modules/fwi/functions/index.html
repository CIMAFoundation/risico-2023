standard_utc_offset_hours
Standard (non-DST) UTC offset in hours of the time zone at (lon, lat): tzf-rs boundaries, IANA rules from chrono-tz 0.10, standard offset = the smaller of the UTC offsets on 15 January and 15 July of the run year (so Europe/Dublin, whose tz rules call summer time “standard” and use a negative winter DST, gets UTC+0 like the rest of the zones without DST in winter); falls back to the nautical zone round(lon / 15) if the zone is unknown.
